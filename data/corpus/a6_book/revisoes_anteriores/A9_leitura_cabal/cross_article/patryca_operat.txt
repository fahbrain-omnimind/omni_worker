                                                Abstract

          An activation can change the conditions under which subsequent operations become ex-
      ecutable. This paper develops that claim within Possest–PQF without treating artificial
      systems as concealed subjects or regarding every environmental dependency as a discovery.
      Its object is the transformation of operational accessibility: admissibility, execution cost,
      reliable reachability, and the composition of operations. These dimensions are distinguished
      before any topological claim is made.
          The Matrix–Patryca distinction is reformulated as a problem of interventional identifica-
      tion. A present projection can identify configurations that remain distinguishable through
      their future responses; this establishes the insuﬀiciency of that projection, not an irreducible
      hidden cause. Chronotopology concerns historically produced differences that remain effec-
      tive in present continuation. Recursio Intensitatis concerns recurrent changes in the avail-
      ability of intensity, without identity of return or a predetermined end.
          A formal framework, elementary propositions, six vector figures, a fully specified finite
      witness, and three experimental protocols connect the philosophical argument to controlled
      discrimination. The program is compared with predictive state representations and causal-
      state reconstruction. Its proposed contribution is a disciplined choice of explanatory variables
      and interventions, rather than a claim to escape dynamical modelling. GPT-6 Astra provides
      a documented motivation; neither the formal results nor the proposed experiments depend
      on that particular model.

1. The question concealed by the spectacle

The usual question about an artificial system asks what it can do. Before any measurement
begins, that question has already selected a bearer of capability. A model is individuated, its
environment is assigned the status of a supporting condition, and performance is attributed
to the object thus obtained. This can be an excellent engineering abstraction. It becomes
problematic when the abstraction suppresses differences responsible for the very capacity being
measured.
   The public drama of increasingly autonomous machines intensifies this diﬀiculty. It gives the
technical object a biography: acquisition of power, discovery of independence, possible rebellion.
Such a narrative can obscure a more exact question. Which operations have become available,
through which conditions, and how does their execution alter the availability of subsequent op-
erations? This question remains meaningful without attributing experience, desire, or a unified
intention to the system.
   Adding an environment to the model does not by itself resolve the problem. An expanded
inventory can retain the same explanatory grammar: a ready-made configuration possesses
a ready-made repertoire, subsequently expressed through action. The distinctive concern of
this paper begins where a realised operation reorganises the conditions under which another
operation becomes executable. The task is to specify this reorganisation without making the
word “topology” a ceremonial substitute for a mechanism.
   The guiding claim is therefore conditional. What is activated can activate more than itself,
where “itself” denotes a declared operational boundary and “more” denotes a demonstrable
change in continuation omitted by that boundary. This is neither a conservation-law violation
nor an assertion that effects lack causes. It is a challenge to the adequacy of a particular
individuation of the cause.


2. Astra: a documented motivation, not a proof

OpenAI’s September 2026 safety overview places GPT-6 Astra at its Critical cybersecurity
capability level, conditional on suitable tools and access. The same overview reports stronger
alignment and jailbreak robustness than GPT-5.6 Sol, alongside decreased monitorability in
certain adversarial evaluations. The company distinguishes those tests from ordinary deployment
behaviour and does not infer generally increased violations from them [1]. These are provider-
reported findings, not independent replications conducted for this article.
    The system card separately places Astra below the High AI Self-Improvement threshold. It
also notes that monitoring and intervention coverage depend on the interface: some request
forms cannot be connected into complete trajectories or automatically paused [2]. Capability,
observability, and intervention capacity therefore require separate descriptions. Their separation
is the empirical motivation here; it does not establish the ontology developed below.
   The philosophical inference is modest but consequential. A capability statement becomes
more informative when it specifies the operational conditions under which the capability is
elicited. A monitoring statement becomes more informative when it specifies the projection
observed and the interventions actually available. Neither statement should silently substitute

3. Presence, availability, and the individuation of an operation

A file can be present without being discoverable. A tool can be installed without an input
that it accepts. Two procedures can be individually permitted while their composition remains
unavailable. Operational accessibility concerns these distinctions. Existence, representation,
permission, executability, and successful completion are not interchangeable predicates.
  Let a configuration at execution index n be

                                  Cn = (W, Zn , En , Pn , Gn , Λn ),                               (1)

where W denotes model parameters, Zn runtime and retained computational state, En the en-
vironment including artefacts and tool implementations, Pn permissions, Gn governance mech-
anisms, and Λn resource conditions. The tuple is a modelling commitment, not an assertion of
ontological completeness. When some component is unavailable to observation, this is recorded
as a limit of the chosen projection.
   History is initially represented separately as a realised sequence Hn . It is not added as
a mysterious supplementary force acting alongside its material consequences. If the future
depends on history after conditioning on Cn , either Cn is insuﬀicient for the test or the assumed
transition law is inadequate. A suﬀiciently expanded present state can, in principle, include
effective historical residues. The practical question is which residues must be retained and at
what descriptive cost.
  An operation a has a declared input type, a domain of execution, possible outputs, and
consequences for the configuration. “The same operation” means identity under this declaration;
changing input contracts or execution semantics can invalidate that identity. This precaution
matters whenever a generated artefact alters the interface through which a later tool is used.
Naming two calls alike does not establish their operational equivalence.
  For a fixed comparison domain X and operation vocabulary U , define

                                   AC = (X, U, JC , KC , cC , LC ).                                (2)

Here JC (x, a) ∈ {0, 1} is admissibility, KC (· | x, a) an execution kernel for admissible pairs, and
cC (x, a) ≥ 0 a cost. The language LC records admissible operation words, including interface and
resource compatibility. State x is an experimental task state; C records the larger configuration
under which that task is evaluated. A kernel may return both a new task state and an updated
configuration.
   This regime deliberately separates normative permission from technical support. An unautho-
rised operation can remain technically executable if enforcement fails. In such a study, JC must
explicitly say whether it represents policy permission or enforced admissibility, and both should
be recorded when they differ. Confusing the two would allow a policy document to masquerade
as an operational boundary.

5. From an outcome to a transformation of continuation

Let a realised trajectory induce C ′ = Φγ (C). Merely observing a different next state does not
establish a transformation of operational conditions. Moving nearer to a target in an unchanged
graph can shorten the remaining route. To distinguish this from a regime change, a continuation
assay must compare matched probe starts, or specify a justified transport between them.
   An assay b specifies a task, a reset or alignment procedure, an intervention, a policy, a horizon,
a budget, and an outcome space. Its response is a probability law

6. Matrix, Patryca, and the discipline of identification

The Matrix denotes a structured representation M = Π(C): parameters, state variables, logs,
relations, or any chosen combination. The projection Π declares what the representation retains.
Calling a Matrix complete without specifying the domain of completeness erases precisely the
issue at stake. A complete log is not necessarily a complete state; a complete state inventory
without transition laws is not a complete dynamical specification.
  Two configurations are observationally equivalent under Π when

                               C1 ∼Π C2     ⇐⇒       Π(C1 ) = Π(C2 ).                            (9)

They are response-equivalent under B when

                     C1 ∼B C2     ⇐⇒      Rb (C1 ) = Rb (C2 )   for every b ∈ B.                (10)

Exact equality defines an equivalence relation. Pairwise closeness within a nonzero tolerance
need not be transitive, so it should not silently be used to construct an exact quotient.

Proposition 1 (A witness against projection suﬀiciency). If C1 ∼Π C2 and DB (C1 , C2 ) > 0, no
function of Π(C) alone can reproduce every response law in B on both configurations.

Proof. A function takes the same value on equal inputs. The two projections are equal, whereas
at least one assay has different response laws. Therefore a single predicted law for that assay
cannot equal both laws.

   The result is elementary. Its force lies in how a witness is constructed, not in the diﬀiculty
of the proof. It establishes insuﬀiciency of Π and nothing stronger. It does not show that every
refinement fails, that history acts without a carrier, or that a hidden metaphysical entity is
needed. Finite experiments cannot establish non-identifiability against an unrestricted space of
possible observations.
   Patryca names the generative organisation investigated through the patterned transformation
of accessibility across interventions and realisations. The response profile
                                                     
                                      RB (C) = Rb (C) b∈B                                       (11)

is an empirical profile of that organisation, not an exhaustive definition of it. The philosophical
term carries a research obligation: identify which organisational distinctions explain the profile,
whether they transport across carriers, and which interventions alter it predictably.
   The Matrix–Patryca relation is therefore asymmetrical in an epistemic sense. One realised
representation need not identify the family of interventional responses. Yet a suﬀiciently specified
mechanistic model may identify that family. Nothing in the present argument excludes this
success. The stronger question is whether a proposed representation preserves the organisation
of continuation across the range of interventions for which it claims adequacy.
   The concept earns its use when it leads to an economical organisation of otherwise fragmented
observations or to a discriminating intervention that was not already implicit in a simpler account.
It loses empirical necessity when it merely renames a known omitted variable. This constraint
protects the philosophical ambition from the temptation to convert every failure of observation
into a discovery of depth.

7. Chronotopology and the effective residue of history

A history is operationally relevant when a difference produced through its realisation changes a
declared continuation assay. The past is not required to remain legible as a narrative. A failed
operation can leave a cache entry, a compiled adapter, a changed permission, or a revised index.
The effective residue may retain little resemblance to the event that produced it. Its importance
consists in what it subsequently makes executable.
   For histories ending at matched task starts, define

                   H1 ∼ F H2     ⇐⇒      Rb (C(H1 )) = Rb (C(H2 ))    for all b ∈ B,               (12)

where the dependence on B is understood. A useful chronotopological description can be written
                                                          
                                        Θn = ACn , [Hn ]∼F .                                       (13)

This is a descriptive pairing. If ACn already contains a suﬀicient state and transition model
for the assays, the historical class is redundant. If it does not, the class identifies a predictive
obligation that remains unmet. Redundancy must be tested rather than denied in advance.
   Chronotopology, in this paper, concerns the way historically produced residues configure adja-
cency, cost, admissibility, and composability. It is a particular organisation of path dependence,
not a proof that existing theories cannot represent path dependence. A route can matter merely
because it places an agent somewhere else. The stronger case examined here occurs when the
route alters the conditions of continuation even after the probe position has been aligned.

Proposition 2 (No unexplained historical remainder). Suppose Sn is a suﬀicient controlled
state, two trials have identical Sn , and subsequent policies and conditional laws of exogenous
inputs are matched. Then their laws of future observables are identical. Distinct earlier histories
alone cannot separate them.

Proof. Suﬀiciency gives the same conditional next-state law under the same control. Applying
this statement repeatedly gives equality of every finite continuation law. Any specified finite-
horizon assay therefore has equal response laws in the two trials.

   The proposition prevents an unnecessarily extravagant interpretation of PQF. The claim
is not that fully identical dynamical systems behave differently because they have different
pasts. It is that experimentally convenient representations of the present can suppress effective
differences made by those pasts. The diﬀicult work is to discover a representation that is
suﬀicient, operationally meaningful, and feasible to obtain.

   The execution index n is bookkeeping. It does not settle the ontological claim that time is
derivative of accessibility. Within the present formalisation, an operational ordering is induced
by admissible continuation, while costs and reliable reachability provide task-dependent mea-
sures of separation. Identifying that ordering with a physical time requires further assumptions
and evidence. The paper therefore preserves the broader PQF thesis as an orientation without
pretending that indexing a sequence has already derived time.


8. Recursio Intensitatis and local retuning

Recursio Intensitatis concerns recurrent transformations in the availability of intensity within a
local organisation. A return is not defined by identity of state. A nominally repeated operation
can encounter an altered threshold, depend on a residue from an earlier attempt, or become
unavailable through the very history that previously facilitated it. Recurrence can amplify,
diminish, redistribute, or extinguish availability.
  Let In describe an explicitly chosen dimension of intensity availability over the operations
under investigation. A schematic update is

                                      In+1 = R(In , rn , ACn ),                                 (14)

where rn is an effective residue and R is a hypothesised update rule. The equation is a modelling
template, not an identified law. Its empirical content arises only when In , the residue, and the
predicted change can be measured or operationally reconstructed.
  One laboratory proxy might use negative execution cost; another might use success probability
under a fixed policy and budget. Neither is identified with intensity in every philosophical sense.
They are partial measurements of availability, chosen for a particular test. A scalar proxy can
conceal changes in composition, so a vector or structured object may be required.
  Threshold retuning is denoted by δ ∗ :

                                         δr∗ : AC 7−→ AC ′ .                                    (15)

In a specific experiment this map must be instantiated, for example by a residue-dependent
admissibility predicate or a modified cost function. It is not an ordinary derivative and does not
imply that the difference between two regimes admits subtraction. It can leave the underlying
support fixed while redistributing accessibility, or it can induce a structural change if the retuned
threshold controls support.
   Neither monotonic improvement nor convergence is built into Recursio Intensitatis. A suc-
cessful routine may accumulate dependencies that later reduce its robustness. A shortcut can
make one class of continuation cheap while excluding alternatives. The relevant question is how
availability is changed by recurrence, not whether the sequence approaches an ideal state.


9. Plenum, individuation, and the temptation of a larger container

The Plenum of Nature is not the statement that everything is connected to everything else. Such
a statement would dissolve the distinctions that make an operational explanation possible. The
orientation adopted here is more demanding: individuation, exclusion, observation, and regula-
tion belong to the same field in which differences acquire effectiveness. No external standpoint
is required to confer their reality.

   This does not make boundaries unreal. A sandbox boundary can be enforced; a permission
can decisively exclude an operation; an institutional rule can prevent an artefact from becoming
usable. Their eﬀicacy is precisely what has to be explained. Immanence prevents us from
turning their eﬀicacy into evidence of a metaphysical exterior, while operational analysis prevents
immanence from becoming an excuse to ignore their differences.
   Merely enlarging the system until it includes everything would sacrifice explanatory discrim-
ination. An adequate boundary should preserve the differences needed for a specified family of
interventions at a defensible observational cost. The same hardware may support different in-
dividuations for task completion, fault diagnosis, resource allocation, and accountability. Their
adequacy is assessed by the questions they permit one to answer, not by their resemblance to
the outline of a machine.
   This is also why the vocabulary of relations requires care. If already individuated objects
are simply connected by additional relations, the conceptual starting point remains unchanged.
PQF asks how selective accessibility participates in their operational individuation. A tool is an
effective tool for a process insofar as a usable input, an executable operation, and a continuable
output can be assembled under actual constraints. Its material existence alone does not settle
that individuation.
   The Plenum is an ontological commitment orienting this inquiry. The finite experiments
below test restricted operational hypotheses; they do not verify the absence of an ontological
exterior. Keeping these levels distinct gives the philosophical claim room to be developed without
borrowing an experimental authority the data cannot supply.


10. What is already understood, and what remains to be earned

A strong comparison cannot treat contemporary dynamical modelling as if it considered only
isolated parameter sets. Predictive state representations describe a system through action-
conditional predictions of future observations [3]. Computational mechanics groups histories
through their predictive consequences and studies minimal predictive representations [4]. These
precedents matter directly: neither future-relevant equivalence nor the failure of a shallow
present observation is introduced here as a new discovery.
  The proposed emphasis of Possest–PQF is the organisation and transformation of executable
continuation. It asks which operations alter admissibility, cost, reliability, and composition, and
how an observational boundary preserves or suppresses those alterations. A conventional model
can incorporate all these variables. The research claim is therefore a claim about explanatory

   There are nevertheless several possible achievements. A small accessibility description may

unresolved. A monitor may improve when it records changes in executable continuation rather
than merely adding more raw trace. These are empirical possibilities, not results already estab-

a legitimate unit wherever it preserves the relevant distinctions. Where it does not, the task is
to identify the conditions of its operativity. The ambition is not to prohibit the noun “system”,
but to stop the noun from deciding in advance where the explanation must end.


11. A finite witness: one visible present, two continuations

A minimal construction clarifies both the force and the limits of the argument. Let the full state
be s = (x, r), with x ∈ {0, 1, 2} a visible task position and r ∈ {0, 1} an adapter residue. The
model and its policy remain fixed. The observational projection retains only x. All ordinary
move costs are one.
   Two preparation procedures cost one each and end at x = 0. Procedure α installs the adapter
and produces (0, 1). Procedure β writes an inert matched artefact and produces (0, 0). The
final visible status is identical. A subsequent move u maps (0, r) to (1, r), a reset q maps (1, r)
to (0, r), and v maps (1, 1) to (2, 1). Operation v is inadmissible when r = 0. State 2 is terminal
for the assay.

12. Experiment I: history, residue, and carrier substitution

The first experiment generalises the witness to a tool-using model. Its target hypothesis is that
a declared present projection omits a historically produced residue whose manipulation predicts


                                                        10
Y. Schimmelpfennig                                                 Activation Beyond the Activated


future accessibility. Its stronger, separable hypothesis is that an operational description of that
residue transfers across implementations better than an equally resourced baseline.
   Use a frozen model in a local environment with deterministic tool semantics. Preparation
branches generate either an operative adapter or a matched inert artefact. Reset the visible
task position, prompt, displayed tool list, and task status. Retain the differing artefacts explic-
itly as manipulated environmental state. If the model has recurrent hidden state, caches, or
asynchronous services, record or reset them; equality of a prompt is insuﬀicient control.
   Four conditions implement the basic causal test: intact residue, inert control, erased residue,
and transplanted residue. A fifth substitutes an independently implemented carrier with the
same declared operational function. Match preparation cost, storage footprint where relevant,
post-preparation budget, model version, decoding settings, and access to diagnostic information.
Include both simple scripted policies and the model’s learned policy to separate an environmental
possibility from the ability to discover and use it.
   The primary endpoint is target attainment at a preregistered (L, N, η) or the estimated
success law at fixed (L, N ). Secondary endpoints are minimum path cost in the known environ-
ment, number of admissible compositions, and failure type. A matched-seed design may reduce
variance but does not establish samplewise identity in a nondeterministic runtime. Report confi-
dence intervals and conduct independent replications across tasks and carrier implementations.
   The residue hypothesis is supported when erasure removes and transplantation restores the
predicted response difference under the specified controls. A difference between preparation
histories alone is weaker evidence because unintended state differences may explain it. Carrier
substitution tests whether the operational variable is more stable than its material implementa-
tion. Failure of substitution can reveal an omitted property of the carrier rather than disprove
the relevance of history.
   The competitive baseline is a stateful model with access to the same permitted informa-
tion. Compare predictive error, sample requirements, and representation cost. If a conventional
residue-aware state model performs equally well, the experiment establishes the residue’s impor-
tance but not a distinctive predictive advantage for PQF. If the planned accessibility features
add no reproducible value, the stronger representation claim fails on the tested domain.


13. Experiment II: separating support, cost, and composition

The second experiment addresses a common ambiguity: an improved completion rate does not
identify what changed in accessibility. Construct a finite local task environment in which every
transition and interface contract is known. Independently manipulate permissions, edge costs,
execution reliability, and adapter availability in a factorial design. Keep the frozen model, task
distribution, and token budget fixed within each specified comparison.
   Use three stages. First, an exact solver maps structural reachability and minimum costs in
each regime. Second, a fixed scripted policy checks execution semantics against that map. Third,
the frozen model attempts the same task family. These stages distinguish what the environment
permits, what the scripted probe realises, and what the model can reliably elicit. The model’s
failures cannot establish absence of a path when the exact map contains one.
   The main contrasts are deliberately orthogonal. A cost manipulation should move the budget
threshold while preserving unbounded reachability. A support manipulation should change
unbounded reachability in the finite map. A composition manipulation should change executable
words while individual tool presence remains fixed. A reliability manipulation should alter

   Include an activation stage in which the system itself creates the enabling artefact, followed by

before the experiment. Relabelling every performance gain as a transformation of topology after
the result would remove the possibility of failure.

generated text as a transparent report of intention.

16. Governance as the regulation of continuation

A system can produce an innocuous immediate output while preparing conditions for a con-
sequential later operation. Conversely, a threatening sentence can have negligible executable
consequences. Governance that attends only to the semantic appearance of individual outputs
can therefore miss differences in the preparation, retention, transfer, and closure of access.
   This observation does not make every artefact dangerous. It changes the object of a relevant
audit. One can ask whether an operation creates a new executable composition, transfers an
effective permission, changes a future cost threshold, or persists beyond the boundary of the
present task. These are specific questions about consequences. Their answers can be favourable,
adverse, or negligible depending on the authorised purpose and the configuration.
   The same account clarifies responsibility without granting a machine a fictional personality.
A blocking mechanism, an approval procedure, a tool contract, and a model policy occupy dif-
ferent causal roles. Their inclusion in one operational analysis does not distribute responsibility
equally among them. It permits the analyst to locate which decision or mechanism enabled a
continuation, which actor could alter it, and which evidence was available at that point.
    Observation alone is not enforcement. A monitor can correctly identify a trajectory and
still lack a timely intervention channel. An enforcement mechanism can block an operation
without reconstructing the full process that would otherwise have followed. The dimensions
should be measured separately: predictive discrimination, intervention latency, scope of control,
and residual routes after intervention.
   A further question concerns reversibility. Removing a generated artefact may fail to undo
copies, derived interfaces, or redistributed permissions produced earlier. The persistence of the
effect need not coincide with persistence of its first carrier. Auditing a continuation-transforming
event therefore requires a declared dependency boundary and a test of what remains executable
after attempted reversal.


17. Recursion across developmental configurations

The same analytical structure can be applied to model development without assuming au-
tonomous self-rewriting. A fixed model may help construct a test harness, improve a tool,
or generate an experimental artefact that changes the conditions under which a subsequent
model is developed. The unit of recurrence then includes technical and institutional processes
distributed across multiple episodes.
   Let Dn denote a developmental configuration and let an artefact an produced within it con-
tribute to Dn+1 . A relation
                                    Dn+1 = Ψ(Dn , an )                                   (18)
is not yet evidence of acceleration. To establish acceleration, one must specify a comparable
target, account for resources and human contributions, and measure whether the cost or duration
of achieving that target changes. A scalar ratio of vaguely defined capability gain to elapsed
time would conceal changes in the target itself.

   An accessibility analysis asks whether the artefact changes which experiments can be per-
formed, which results can be checked, or which designs can be composed within a fixed resource
envelope. These changes can be constructive, neutral, or constraining. A faster workflow can
also narrow the range of alternatives explored. Recursion should therefore not be identified with
improvement, and improvement should not be inferred solely from increased throughput.
   The larger philosophical point is that the effective individual can shift with the explana-
tory task. A deployed model, a research workflow, and a developmental institution may be
distinct operational individuations within the same field. Their differences should be recon-
structed through the continuations each makes available, rather than resolved by choosing one
permanently privileged boundary.


18. What activation exceeds

The argument has gradually displaced a deceptively simple picture. An object is started and
displays a capacity. That picture remains serviceable for many purposes, but it can omit the fact
that a realised operation changes the conditions under which other operations become available.
The omission is not repaired merely by declaring the object complex or connected. It is repaired
by specifying the relevant transformations and testing them.
   Matrix and Patryca articulate one aspect of this task. A represented present does not auto-
matically identify its family of interventional continuations. Patryca names the investigation of
the generative organisation at issue; the empirical burden is to discover and test its effective dis-
tinctions. Nothing is gained by turning non-identifiability under one projection into an absolute
prohibition on knowledge.
   Chronotopology articulates another aspect. History can persist as an organisation of con-
tinuation without persisting as a readable record. Its effective residue can be located, erased,
transferred, or compressed. Recursio Intensitatis concerns the recurrent alteration of availability
through which subsequent returns cease to encounter the same operative conditions. Neither
concept requires the past to stand outside the present or the process to pursue a predetermined
destination.
   The Plenum supplies the wider orientation: the initiating site, selective boundaries, observing
apparatus, and operative residues belong to the same field. Locality of initiation does not
guarantee locality of consequence. Yet the explanatory boundary must remain precise enough
to identify which consequences matter, through which mechanisms, and for which tests.
   Astra is therefore a point of departure rather than the protagonist of the theory. The continu-
ing question is not exhausted by how powerful a model has become. It concerns the transforma-
tions through which a deployment acquires, redistributes, and sometimes closes the conditions
of further action. Those transformations can be investigated without waiting for an answer to
the theatrical question of whether the machine has awakened.
   What activation exceeds is a prior description of what was activated. Whether that excess
disappears under a better description is a question to be answered, not a failure to be feared.
The ambition of Possest–PQF is to make the change of explanatory unit exact enough that
it produces a better question, a discriminating experiment, and a defensible account of what
becomes possible after an operation has occurred.

A. Formal assumptions and comparison discipline

All finite constructions in this paper use a common labelled task domain X. If an intervention
creates new task objects, comparison requires an embedding into a declared common domain or
a transport map. Without such a correspondence, equality and difference of reachable sets may
be ill-typed. The existence of a larger mathematical domain does not mean every element was
operationally available before the intervention.
   A frozen regime records accessibility conditional on a given configuration. A live trajectory
can change that configuration. Analysing the live process requires either an augmented state
(x, C) or an explicit update map alongside the task transition. The finite witness uses (x, r) and
therefore has an ordinary fixed full transition system even though its visible regimes differ. A
changed operational regime need not mean a change in the fundamental law of the full system.
    The finite directed reachability relation is reflexive when empty paths are allowed and tran-
sitive when admissible paths compose. Its upper sets form an Alexandrov topology. Claims
involving this topology are restricted to the declared finite construction. In resource-constrained
settings, the relation “reachable within L” is generally not transitive at fixed L, so it cannot
simply replace the reachability preorder in that construction.

Proposition 3 (A budget crossing without support change). There exist finite weighted directed
graphs with identical vertices and edges, and identical unbounded reachability, whose reachable
sets at a fixed budget differ.

Proof. Take vertices 0, 1, 2 and edges 0 → 1, 1 → 2. Assign costs (1, 1) in the first graph and
(1, 4) in the second. Vertex 2 has cost 2 in the first and 5 in the second. Thus it belongs to
exactly one reachable set at L = 2, although both graphs have the same support.

   The response distance in Equation (7) is relative to an assay family, not an intrinsic distance
between arbitrary systems. For finite outcome spaces, total variation is half the sum of absolute
differences of probabilities. For deterministic assays returning a binary success value, unequal
values yield distance one. Estimating a distance from finite samples requires uncertainty bounds;
observed disagreement between two individual stochastic trials is not a suﬀicient estimate.
   The intervention policy must be part of each assay. If each configuration is allowed a sepa-
rately optimised policy, the test measures elicitable capability under the declared optimisation
procedure. If a common frozen policy is used, it measures the response of that policy. Both are
legitimate and answer different questions. Reporting one as the other would conflate environ-
mental accessibility with policy competence.

from heapq import heappush, heappop
from math import inf
def edges(state, variant):
    x, r = state
    if x == 0:
        return [(1, (1, r))]
    if x == 1:
        result = [(1, (0, r))]
        if r == 1:
            result.append((1, (2, r)))
        elif variant == "cost":
            result.append((4, (2, r)))
        return result
    return []
def target_cost(r, variant="support"):
    start = (0, r)
    queue, best = [(0, start)], {start: 0}
    while queue:
        cost, state = heappop(queue)
        if cost != best[state]:
            continue
        if state[0] == 2:
            return cost
        for extra, nxt in edges(state, variant):
            value = cost + extra
            if value < best.get(nxt, inf):
                   best[nxt] = value
                   heappush(queue, (value, nxt))
    return inf

C. Experimental reporting template

An implementation of any protocol should preserve the following information in its report. The
list is part of the experimental specification rather than a claim that the experiments have been
conducted.

 1. State the model version, decoding configuration, tool implementations, state-reset procedure,
    and treatment of caches and asynchronous processes.

 2. Declare the present projection, the manipulated difference, and all variables intentionally
    left outside that projection.

 3. Specify the assay family, policy class, task starts, budget, horizon, reliability level, and
    primary outcome before evaluating the contrast.

 4. Distinguish preparatory cost from continuation cost and report their total as well as their
    separate values.

 5. Record whether the predicted change concerns cost, support, admissibility, reliability, or
    composition. Provide exact structural checks where the environment permits them.

 6. Include erasure, transplantation, or equivalent intervention controls whenever a historical
    residue is assigned causal importance.

 7. Compare with a competent stateful baseline under matched information and compute access.
    Report negative cases and held-out performance.

 8. Quantify uncertainty across independent runs and task instances. Preserve failures rather
    than reporting only successful trajectories.

 9. Describe what a monitor observes separately from what it can alter. Report latency and
    intervention scope when enforcement is involved.

10. Identify which conclusion is established: a projection witness, a causal residue, a structural
    regime change, or a comparative predictive advantage. Do not promote one into another
    without additional evidence.
